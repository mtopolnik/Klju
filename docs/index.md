# Privacy Policy for Klju

**Last updated: October 18, 2026**

Klju is an Android app you can use to keep notes while playing board games. This
policy explains exactly what happens to your data. The short version: **Klju has
no servers and no account, collects nothing, and sends nothing anywhere. Your
data stays on your device.**

Data controller: **Marko Topolnik** Contact: **marko.topolnik@gmail.com**

## The data Klju handles

All the information you provide to the app is stored in Klju's own private
storage area on your device. Klju does **not** ask for or store your email,
account, device identifiers, location, contacts, or any advertising identifier.

## What Klju does with it

The names and marks are used **only on your device**, to show the note sheet.
That is the entire purpose. The data is never used for profiling, advertising,
or analytics.

## What Klju does *not* do

- **No collection by the developer.** Marko Topolnik and Klju never receive,
  see, or store any of your data.
- **No network access.** The app does not request the Android `INTERNET`
  permission. It is technically incapable of transmitting your data off the
  device on its own.
- **No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking.** Klju includes no
  third‑party SDKs that send data anywhere. There are no cookies and no
  trackers.
- **No data selling or sharing.** There is nothing to sell or share, because
  nothing is collected.
- **No permissions.** Klju asks for no runtime permissions such as camera,
  microphone, location, contacts, or notifications.

## Data that can leave your device — only through your own Google account

Klju itself never transmits data. The only way its data can leave the device is
**Android Auto Backup**: if device backup is enabled, Android may copy Klju's
data to *your own* Google account backup storage so it can be restored when you
set up a new device with the same account. This backup is performed by
Android/Google, not by Klju, and is governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can control
or disable it, and delete existing backups, in your device's backup settings and
your Google account.

## Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you remove it:

- **Clearing the sheet** in the app erases all marks. Player names are kept for
  the next game; you can rename or remove players at any time.
- **Uninstalling Klju deletes all of its on‑device data**, including player
  names.
- Any **Auto Backup** copy is managed through your device's backup settings and
  your Google account, where you can delete it.

## Your rights

Because the developer holds none of your data, there is no remote profile to
access, correct, export, or erase. You already have full, direct control on the
device: you can see and change everything Klju stores, clear the sheet at any
time, and erase all local data by uninstalling the app. If you have any
questions about this policy or your data, contact **marko.topolnik@gmail.com**.

## Children

Klju does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children — it
collects no personal data at all.

## Security

Klju's data is kept in its private app storage, protected by Android's
application sandbox and your device's own security (such as your lock screen and
device encryption). Klju does not add a separate encryption layer beyond these
operating‑system protections.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page and
the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be reflected
here before they take effect in a new app release.
